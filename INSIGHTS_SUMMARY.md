# Executive Insights Report: Global Olympic Sports Performance (1992–2026)

## Scenario 1: Priya – National Olympic Committee (NOC) Performance Strategist
- **High-Yield Discipline Targeting:** Analysis of medals per athlete reveals stark productivity variance across disciplines. Technical categories with smaller squad sizes—specifically **Rowing (6.916 medals/athlete)** and **Shooting (6.731 medals/athlete)**—yield significantly higher podium returns per accreditation than large-contingent team sports (2.150 medals/athlete).
- **Strategic Recommendation for Priya:** Reallocate national high-performance funding away from low-conversion team rosters toward targeted micro-squads in Shooting, Rowing, and Combat Sports, maximizing national medal count per traveling contingent.

## Scenario 2: Carlos – Sports Investment & Funding Agency Analyst
- **Overperforming Low-Budget Outliers:** The capital-efficiency matrix isolates high-yield nations that produce world-class athletes at a fraction of Tier-1 budgets. Nations such as **Slovakia, Georgia, and Ethiopia** demonstrate exceptional medal-to-dollar conversion, spending under \$15M annually while consistently securing podium placements.
- **Regional Efficiency Allocation:** South America demonstrates the highest regional medal conversion efficiency (**49.64%**), closely followed by Europe and Asia (**45.98%**).
- **Strategic Recommendation for Carlos:** Prioritize grassroots infrastructure grants in high-efficiency regions (South America and Eastern Europe) where marginal funding generates exponential medal gains compared to saturated Western sports ecosystems.
